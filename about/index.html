About This Site

Straight answers for dog people, without the gatekeeping.

Doggy See, Doggy Do exists because getting a straight answer about your dog online is unreasonably hard. Every search turns into ten tabs, four contradictory opinions, and a forum thread from 2011. We wanted one place where the answer is just there.

Who publishes this site

Doggy See, Doggy Do is a faceless editorial brand. We keep the focus on useful answers rather than personalities, and we do not use invented expert profiles or borrowed credentials. We are not veterinarians and we do not pretend to be. Our responsibility is to make our process visible: what sources we use, where our limits are, and how we correct the record.

How the content gets made

  • We start from published veterinary and professional training guidance, not from forum consensus.
  • We write for the average healthy dog and say so when a situation falls outside that.
  • We do not diagnose, and we never tell you to override your own veterinarian.
  • Every article carries a publication date so you can judge how current it is.
  • When we get something wrong and find out, we fix it rather than quietly leaving it up.

Read the complete Editorial Standards and Corrections Policy.

What we will not do

We will not tell you your dog has a specific condition. We will not talk you out of a vet visit. We will not recommend a product because someone paid us to, and we will not leave a recommendation up once we stop believing in it. If a company wants coverage here, the answer is that coverage is not for sale.

How the site pays for itself

Some links here are or will become affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission when you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. The full explanation is on our Affiliate Disclosure page, and it is worth two minutes of your time.

Say hello

Questions, corrections, and "you got this wrong" emails are all welcome at doggysee.doggydo@yahoo.com. Corrections especially.
